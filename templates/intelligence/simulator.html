Formulas Used & Mathematical Methodology
Compounding Projection Formulas
  • 1. Net Monthly Compounding Rate:
    Net_Growth_Rate = (Customer_Growth_% - Churn_%) / 100
  • 2. Month 1 MRR (Initial Price & Growth Shift):
    MRR_1 = Base_MRR × (1 + Price_Increase_% / 100) × (1 + Net_Growth_Rate)
  • 3. Month t MRR (Compound Recurrence):
    MRR_t = MRR_(t-1) × (1 + Net_Growth_Rate) for t = 2, 3, ..., 12
Annualization & Cumulative Impact
  • 4. Month 12 Projected ARR:
    ARR_12 = MRR_12 × 12
  • 5. 12-Month Cumulative Cash Inflow:
    Cumulative_Revenue = ∑(MRR_1 + MRR_2 + ... + MRR_12)
  • 6. Incremental Impact vs Baseline Status Quo:
    Variance = Cumulative_Revenue - (Base_MRR × 12)
